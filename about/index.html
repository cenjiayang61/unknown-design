02 — Approach方法

Every brand gets its own model — never a borrowed style.

  1. 01Reasoning before style先推导，后风格

    我们不始于风格，而始于严谨的商业逻辑与市场推导：拆解品牌内核、竞争环境与用户心智，提炼可被空间转译的符号与动线。

    We begin with commercial logic, not a style — taking apart the brand's core, its competition and its customers, and distilling what a space can carry.

  2. 02One brand, one model一个品牌，一个模型

    为每个品牌单独研发落地模型，让空间成为品牌故事的载体，而非装饰的堆砌。

    For every brand we develop a model of its own, so the space carries the brand's story rather than a pile of decoration.

  3. 03Resetting the scene重设消费场景

    在品牌与人的每一个触点上，策略、视觉、空间、材质、灯光乃至气味，同频共振。

    Wherever a brand meets its people — strategy, identity, space, material, light, even scent, all in one key.

04 — Identity标识

u + n

一扇门，一束光，一个空间。

图标取自字母 u 与 n：n 是一扇门，从门外照入的一束光塑造了 u 的空间，让图标从二维平面成为三维空间。

The n is a door; a beam of light through it shapes the space of the u — turning a flat sign into a room.
