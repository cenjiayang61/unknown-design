About

A brand’s space is never just an enclosure — it is a slice of how the brand sees the world. We work on its structure, its rhythm and its language. Structure is the skeleton, rhythm is the breath, language is the tone.

Unknown Design is an independent spatial design studio founded in Shenzhen in 2019, working across brand identity and commercial space.

Since 2019

We do not begin with style. We begin with rigorous commercial logic and market reasoning — unpacking a brand’s core, its competition and its audience, and distilling the symbols and journeys that space can carry.

For every brand we develop a model of its own. Space becomes the carrier of a brand’s story, not a pile of decoration — and the model has to hold from the first sketch to the last light fitting.

We are good at resetting consumer scenes. At every touchpoint between a brand and its people, we build places with narrative tension and commercial effect — keeping strategy, visual, space, material and even scent in the same key.t along the way. In the end, we let the work speak for itself.

We respect the fields we do not know, and keep analyzing and thinking. We explore unknown possibilities, and keep practicing and concluding.

Three things guide our taste: contradiction, which begins in surprise and earns attention; craft, which begins in judgement — scale, material and place; and warmth, which begins in everyday life, and earns affection. from project to project.

From a client, at the end of a long project:

“The struggle, the heartache, and the grueling long hours are all prerequisites for running a small studio.

On the flip side are lasting friendships and many hours of laughter.

Without patience, there is no craft. And without craft, there is no reward. These are the things we learned from our teachers over the years.”

A practice across brand identity and spatial design — the stories that make up a studio, and the unknowns we will keep exploring…

With thanks.

Shenzhen · Since 2019
