Our trip 
across China

Stops along the way · 一路走来的项目站点 →

从第一张草图到最终交付，我们穿行在甜品店、茶舍与餐厅之间，为每一个空间找到它自己的故事。
From first sketch to final delivery, we crossed dessert shops, teahouses and restaurants in search of each room’s own story.
24 completed projects 2019
/2026

这些项目给了我们慢下来的时间——长久的驻场、一个个小决定，让空间慢慢长出自己的颜色。
These projects gave us the gift of slow time: long site visits and small decisions colouring each space.

每一个委托都从经典问题出发，再加入属于场地的答案：招牌、灯光、家具与菜单。
Every brief started from a classic problem and grew site-specific answers: signage, lighting, furniture and menus.

在城市之间穿行是最美的部分——视野打开，天空变大，每一个新场地都是一次自由的开始。
Driving between cities was the beautiful part: the view opening up, the sky bigger, every new site a fresh start.

The Stops

#1

JMC Garden

— Foshan Nanhai
Area
75 m²

JMC Garden aspires to be a restaurant that grows out of a garden. Greenery leads from the entrance deep into the room, anchored by dark, steady timber, while low light lands only on tables and plants — a private garden at dusk.

“想做一间「长在花园里的餐厅」——绿意从门口一路引向深处，深色的沉稳木质托住整间店，绿植在上方与转角肆意生长，像把户外的花园搬进了室内。”

Completed 2026

Garden · Dining

#2

Legent Bar

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
100 m²

A bar for the night, themed on liquor and poker. The tension of the poker table is translated into space: dark timber and leather, shelves in geometric order, amber pools of light over the bar.

“把赌桌般的紧张与克制翻译成空间的语言：深色木质与皮革，沉稳而略带侵略性；灯光压得很低，只在吧台与卡座投下琥珀色的光，每一杯酒都带上私密的对峙感。”

Completed 2026

Liquor & Poker

#3

小西家作 · 万象城

— Guangzhou Mixc
Area
65 m²

The keyword of the Mixc store is a single continuous arc. A curved timber counter rolls like a soft wave from the facade inward, guiding the eye and the flow; the ceiling answers in the same line, easing the rigidity of the mall unit.

“万象城店的关键词，是一条贯穿始终的弧——弧形木质柜台如一道柔和的波浪，从门头延伸进室内，吊顶以同源的曲线呼应，消解商场铺位的方正与冷感。”

Completed 2026

Follow the curve

#4

贰轮甜室 · 顺德

— Foshan Shunde
Area
75 m²

The Shunde store follows a Japanese family woodwork style. Warm shelves, tables and chairs of timber — the shop reads as a gentle, familiar house, and the light is warm and low, like a nightlight at home.

“温润的木架、木桌、木椅，连空气里都像浮着一层淡淡的木香；灯光暖而低，像家里的夜灯——坐下，点一份甜品，就像回到一个熟悉而温暖的地方。”

Completed 2026

Like coming home

#5

乐胖子手工pizza

— Guilin
Area
75 m²

The core of Le Pangzi is “handmade,” so we placed the oven and the craft in the most visible spot. Brick and wood in rough texture, warm orange light — seating wraps around the production area, from dough to flame in full view.

“乐胖子的核心是「手工」二字，我们便把窑炉与手作摆到最显眼的位置：砖与木的粗粝质感，配合暖橙的灯光，座位围绕制作区，让人看着一张饼从面团到出炉。”

Completed 2026

Honest food by the fire

#6

小西家作 · 庙前西街

— Guangzhou Miaoqian Xijie
Area
75 m²

For the Miaoqian Xijie shop, we focused on the hexagonal tiles underfoot and the brass at your fingertips. Instead of piling on ornament, we let the materials speak — the order of tile, the lustre of brass, the warmth of timber.

“庙前西街店，重点落在脚下的六角砖与指尖的黄铜上。我们不用繁复的装饰去铺陈，而让材质本身成为主角——砖的秩序、铜的光泽、木的温润。”

Completed 2026

Materials as ornament

#7

贰轮甜室 · 禅城

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
150 m²

Not a single shop but two adjacent storefronts renovated together into a “twin” space: a Showa-style dessert room on one side, a Southeast-Asian stir-fry on the other. Material and light carry the transition, so in a few steps a guest moves from dessert to a table of stir-fry.

“禅城店并非一间铺子，而是同时租下两个相邻铺面一起改造的「双生」空间：一边是昭和风情的贰轮甜室，一边是东南亚风味的浪漫小炒，两道门面并肩而立，各有各的温度。”

Completed 2026

Two doors, two moods

#8

小西家作 · 亭台

— Guangzhou Taikoo Li
Area
80 m²

“Pavilion” borrows the spirit of an Eastern garden structure — a place to pause, to gaze, to rest. Rattan, cord and timber are woven into a light skeleton, and the space breathes between openness and enclosure.

“「亭台」，取自东方园林里那一座可停、可望、可休憩的构筑。编藤、编绳与木构的经纬织出轻盈的骨架，让空间在通透与围合之间呼吸。”

Completed 2026

A woven pavilion

#9

顾涌兴

— Shanghai Panlong
Area
50 m²

Set in Panlong, the old bricks, tiles and beams stay as the base while modern furnishing and clear light breathe through — the old is the foundation, the new is the breath.

“老旧的砖瓦与木梁保留下来，成为空间的底色；现代的陈设与通透的光线轻轻加进去——我们不在新与旧之间取舍，旧的是底气，新的是呼吸。”

Completed 2026

Old meets new

#10

Mini Bar

— Qingyuan
Area
400 m²

A coffee and music lounge hidden in a French-rustic country courtyard. Its charm lies precisely in being hidden — rustic brick, reclaimed wood and old objects stack up the traces of time.

“穿过绿植与斑驳的墙面，才在庭院深处寻到这方小天地。复古的砖、木与旧物堆叠出时间的痕迹，一盏暖灯、一把藤椅，把这里调成可以发呆整晚的角落。”

Completed 2025

Hidden, then found

#11

初拾山

— Foshan
Area
75 m²

Chushishan is a refined Guizhou restaurant, and we invited the imagery of “mountains” together with Guizhou's earthy warmth into the room. Rough stone and wood keep their raw grain, while rows of lanterns glow above the tables like a mountain village at night.

“初拾山是一间贵州本味精致餐厅，我们把「山」的意象与贵州的烟火气一起请进店里：粗犷的石与木呼应大地的质朴，成排的灯笼吊灯像山间村落的灯火，温暖而笃定。”

Completed 2025

Rustic, yet refined

#12

小西家作 · 云朵

— Guangzhou Ali Center
Area
75 m²

In an office district defined by heavy, impersonal energy, we introduced a “cloud” — a French-retro dessert space where people can step away and breathe. Light, fluid and curved, the cloud softens the site's rigid lines, so a slice of cake and a good cup of tea can ease the tension of the day.

“云朵轻盈、可变、曲态，我们用它去化解场地不可变的硬朗线条，让一件蛋糕的甜与一杯好茶的香，卸下些许紧绷。”

Completed 2025

A cloud to unbend

#13

nini cucina

— Guilin
Area
30 m²

The most striking thing about nini cucina is its sawtooth roof — no ornament but the space's protagonist, letting daylight pour through high sloped windows and trace folding lines of light and shadow. By night it becomes a gently glowing box.

“nini cucina 最醒目的是一道锯齿形屋顶——它不是装饰，而是空间的主角：天光从斜面高窗倾泻而入，勾画出一道道光与影的折线；到了夜晚，暖光从齿间透出，建筑变成一盏温柔发光的盒子。”

Completed 2025

Under the sawtooth roof

#14

蕉Pho

— Foshan Nanhai
Area
80 m²

Jiao Pho is a Vietnamese pho shop, and with tiles of blue we set a refreshing keynote — the blue of Vietnam's coast and indigo-dyed cloth, cool, bright and relaxed.

“一块块蓝砖为它定下清爽的基调——那抹蓝是越南海岸与靛蓝染布的颜色。蓝砖从墙面铺到吧台，与木与白相配，像一碗 pho 里的青柠与薄荷，提神而不抢戏。”

Completed 2025

Coast blue, lime bright

#15

如初见

— Guangzhou
Area
75 m²

Ruchujian is centered on nourishing sweet soups and buns, and what we keep is the gentle warmth of a first meeting. No deliberate aging — only plain materials and fitting blank space, letting steam and sweetness speak for themselves.

“如初见以滋补糖水与包点为核心，我们想留住的正是「初见」那一眼的温润。不刻意做旧，只用素净的材质与得当的留白，让热气与甜香自己去说话。”

Completed 2025

Warmth at first sight

#16

HOLY CREAM

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
75 m²

HOLY CREAM is a refined dessert brand for the young, and what we built is a pink dream worth photographing. Rounded arches and globe lights make the space feel like softly piped cream, plump and gentle.

“低饱和的粉不甜腻，反而带着一丝奶油般的高级感；圆润的拱形与球形灯具，让空间像一块被轻轻挤出的奶油，饱满而柔和。”

Completed 2024

Sweet, never cloying

#17

nini brunch

— Guilin
Area
75 m²

nini brunch invites you to finish a leisurely brunch between stone walls and timber frames. The bare stone brings a raw texture, wooden frames catch the morning light grid by grid, and generous seating sets an unhurried pace.

“nini brunch 想让人在石墙与木框之间，慢悠悠地吃完一顿早午餐。裸露的石墙带来粗粝质感，木框让晨光一格一格地漏进来，宽适的桌椅提醒你——不必赶。”

Completed 2024

No need to hurry

#18

兰真真清真包子甜食铺

— Shanghai
Area
85 m²

Lan Zhenzhen is a halal baozi and dessert shop, and through a grid of green we found it an expression both traditional and approachable — the old shop in a new language.

“一格格绿，从门头延伸到窗扇与柜面，带着老字号般的熟悉感；木与暖白铺陈其间，让绿色沉稳下来——绿格，是记忆里的老铺子，也是这间店的新语言。”

Completed 2024

Old shop, new language

#19

半山茶舍

— Foshan Shunde
Area
85 m²

At Banshan Teahouse, the “arc” is a gentle stroke throughout: soft curves dissolve the stiffness of straight lines like slopes rising and falling on a mountainside. The designer's hand-drawn sketches stay quietly in the room as part of the space.

“半山茶舍，我们让「弧」成为贯穿始终的温柔笔触——柔和的弧形消解直线的生硬，像山间缓缓起伏的坡线；设计师的手绘草图也留在空间里，成为一处不经意的巧思。”

Completed 2024

Halfway up the hill

#20

隐幽茶舍

— Foshan Shunde
Area
80 m²

“Yinyou” — hidden quiet — is the teahouse's name and the very mood we wanted to build: to hide away, to settle down. With dim light, steady wood and airy partitions, we filter out the noise outside layer by layer, leaving a quiet room for solitude.

“「隐幽」，是茶舍的名字，也是最想营造的意境——藏起来，静下来。幽暗的光、沉稳的木与通透的隔断，把外面的喧嚣一层层滤掉，只留下一方可供独处的静室。”

Completed 2024

隐幽茶舍 · Tea & Light

#21

olayks

— Shanghai
Area
120 m²

An exhibition booth that builds a small appliance brand a temporary home in a matter of days. Each product is placed in its own life scene, so a visitor understands at a glance where it belongs.

“展台虽小，动线却流畅——浏览、体验、洽谈，一气呵成。我们要的不是一个喧闹的展位，而是一个能让产品自己说话、让人愿意停下来的安静舞台。”

Completed 2024

Scenes, not shelves

#22

稻壳语麦芽

— Foshan Shunde
Area
200 m²

“Rice husk” and “malt” are the code hidden in the shop's name. Warm wheat-yellow tones, plain grained textures and light like a field at dusk quietly tell the story of field and barn.

“木的纹理、编织的纹理、略带颗粒感的墙面，像把一缕缕麦香揉进了空间里；灯光如暮色里的麦田，温暖而饱满。”

Completed 2024

Fields and barns

#23

chuchulab

— Guangzhou Polytrade
Area
120 m²

Built around the Japanese shortcake — small, refined and just enough. White and pale wood form the base, and the counter puts the patissier's craft on quiet show.

“短蛋糕，是一种小而精、恰到好处的甜，我们的空间也依循同样的逻辑——克制、精准、不多不少。”

Completed 2024

Just enough

#24

soho !

— Foshan Nanhai
Area
75 m²

A coffee and light-meal space whose tone is set by a single colour: mint green, flowing from the facade inward, softened by pale wood, rounded furniture and gentle curves.

“这里不讲究厚重，只讲究「舒服」——像一口清爽的薄荷，让人一走进来就放松下来。”

Completed 2023

A breath of mint
