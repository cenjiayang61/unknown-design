Our trip 
across China

Stops along the way · 一路走来的项目站点 →

从第一张草图到最终交付，我们穿行在甜品店、茶舍与餐厅之间，为每一个空间找到它自己的故事。From first sketch to final delivery, we crossed dessert shops, teahouses and restaurants in search of each room’s own story.
24 completed projects 2019
/2026

这些项目给了我们慢下来的时间——长久的驻场、一个个小决定，让空间慢慢长出自己的颜色。These projects gave us the gift of slow time: long site visits and small decisions colouring each space.

每一个委托都从经典问题出发，再加入属于场地的答案：招牌、灯光、家具与菜单。Every brief started from a classic problem and grew site-specific answers: signage, lighting, furniture and menus.

在城市之间穿行是最美的部分——视野打开，天空变大，每一个新场地都是一次自由的开始。Driving between cities was the beautiful part: the view opening up, the sky bigger, every new site a fresh start.

The Stops

#1

JMC Garden

— Foshan Nanhai
Area
75 m²

JMC Garden aspires to be a restaurant that grows out of a garden. Greenery leads from the entrance deep into the room, anchored by dark, steady timber, while low light lands only on tables and plants — a private garden at dusk.

“想做一间「长在花园里的餐厅」——绿意从门口一路引向深处，深色的沉稳木质托住整间店，绿植在上方与转角肆意生长，像把户外的花园搬进了室内。”

Since 2026

Garden · Dining

#2

Legent Bar

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
100 m²

A bar for the night, themed on liquor and poker. The tension of the poker table is translated into space: dark timber and leather, shelves in geometric order, amber pools of light over the bar.

“把赌桌般的紧张与克制翻译成空间的语言：深色木质与皮革，沉稳而略带侵略性；灯光压得很低，只在吧台与卡座投下琥珀色的光，每一杯酒都带上私密的对峙感。”

Since 2026

Bartender

#3

贰轮甜室 · 顺德

— Foshan Shunde
Area
75 m²

The Shunde store follows a Japanese family woodwork style. Warm shelves, tables and chairs of timber — the shop reads as a gentle, familiar house, and the light is warm and low, like a nightlight at home.

“温润的木架、木桌、木椅，连空气里都像浮着一层淡淡的木香；灯光暖而低，像家里的夜灯——坐下，点一份甜品，就像回到一个熟悉而温暖的地方。”

Since 2026

Like coming home

#4

顾涌兴

— Shanghai Panlong
Area
50 m²

Set in Panlong, the old bricks, tiles and beams stay as the base while modern furnishing and clear light breathe through — the old is the foundation, the new is the breath.

“老旧的砖瓦与木梁保留下来，成为空间的底色；现代的陈设与通透的光线轻轻加进去——我们不在新与旧之间取舍，旧的是底气，新的是呼吸。”

Since 2026

Old meets new

#5

Mini Bar

— Qingyuan
Area
400 m²

A coffee and music lounge hidden in a French-rustic country courtyard. Its charm lies precisely in being hidden — rustic brick, reclaimed wood and old objects stack up the traces of time.

“穿过绿植与斑驳的墙面，才在庭院深处寻到这方小天地。复古的砖、木与旧物堆叠出时间的痕迹，一盏暖灯、一把藤椅，把这里调成可以发呆整晚的角落。”

Since 2025

Hidden, then found

#6

小西家作 · 云朵

— Guangzhou Ali Center
Area
75 m²

In an office district defined by heavy, impersonal energy, we introduced a “cloud” — a French-retro dessert space where people can step away and breathe. Light, fluid and curved, the cloud softens the site's rigid lines, so a slice of cake and a good cup of tea can ease the tension of the day.

“云朵轻盈、可变、曲态，我们用它去化解场地不可变的硬朗线条，让一件蛋糕的甜与一杯好茶的香，卸下些许紧绷。”

Since 2025

A cloud to unbend

#7

蕉Pho

— Foshan Nanhai
Area
80 m²

Jiao Pho is a Vietnamese pho shop, and with tiles of blue we set a refreshing keynote — the blue of Vietnam's coast and indigo-dyed cloth, cool, bright and relaxed.

“一块块蓝砖为它定下清爽的基调——那抹蓝是越南海岸与靛蓝染布的颜色。蓝砖从墙面铺到吧台，与木与白相配，像一碗 pho 里的青柠与薄荷，提神而不抢戏。”

Since 2025

Coast blue, lime bright

#8

隐幽茶舍

— Foshan Shunde
Area
80 m²

“Yinyou” — hidden quiet — is the teahouse's name and the very mood we wanted to build: to hide away, to settle down. With dim light, steady wood and airy partitions, we filter out the noise outside layer by layer, leaving a quiet room for solitude.

“「隐幽」，是茶舍的名字，也是最想营造的意境——藏起来，静下来。幽暗的光、沉稳的木与通透的隔断，把外面的喧嚣一层层滤掉，只留下一方可供独处的静室。”

Since 2024

隐幽茶舍 · Tea & Light

#9

HOLY CREAM

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
75 m²

HOLY CREAM is a refined dessert brand for the young, and what we built is a pink dream worth photographing. Rounded arches and globe lights make the space feel like softly piped cream, plump and gentle.

“低饱和的粉不甜腻，反而带着一丝奶油般的高级感；圆润的拱形与球形灯具，让空间像一块被轻轻挤出的奶油，饱满而柔和。”

Since 2024

Sweet, never cloying

#10

兰真真清真包子甜食铺

— Shanghai
Area
85 m²

Lan Zhenzhen is a halal baozi and dessert shop, and through a grid of green we found it an expression both traditional and approachable — the old shop in a new language.

“一格格绿，从门头延伸到窗扇与柜面，带着老字号般的熟悉感；木与暖白铺陈其间，让绿色沉稳下来——绿格，是记忆里的老铺子，也是这间店的新语言。”

Since 2024

Old shop, new language
