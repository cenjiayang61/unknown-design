Our trip 
across China

Stops along the way · 一路走来的项目站点 →

从第一张草图到最终交付，我们穿行在甜品店、茶舍与餐厅之间，为每一个空间找到它自己的故事。From first sketch to final delivery, we crossed dessert shops, teahouses and restaurants in search of each room’s own story.
24 completed projects 2023
/2026

这些项目给了我们慢下来的时间——长久的驻场、一个个小决定，让空间慢慢长出自己的颜色。These projects gave us the gift of slow time: long site visits and small decisions colouring each space.

每一个委托都从经典问题出发，再加入属于场地的答案：招牌、灯光、家具与菜单。Every brief started from a classic problem and grew site-specific answers: signage, lighting, furniture and menus.

#1

Xiaoxi Jiazuo

— Guangzhou Ali Center
Area
75 m²

In an office district defined by heavy, impersonal energy, we introduced a “cloud” — a French-retro dessert space where people can step away and breathe. Light, fluid and curved, the cloud softens the site's rigid lines, so a slice of cake and a good cup of tea can ease the tension of the day.

“云朵轻盈、可变、曲态，我们用它去化解场地不可变的硬朗线条，让一件蛋糕的甜与一杯好茶的香，卸下些许紧绷。”

Since 2025

Client · Dessert

在城市之间穿行是最美的部分——视野打开，天空变大，每一个新场地都是一次自由的开始。Driving between cities was the beautiful part: the view opening up, the sky bigger, every new site a fresh start.

“Yinyou” — hidden quiet — is the teahouse's name and the very mood we wanted to build: to hide away, to settle down. With dim light, steady wood and airy partitions, we filter out the noise outside layer by layer, leaving a quiet room for solitude.

#3

HOLY CREAM

— Foshan Chancheng
Area
75 m²

HOLY CREAM is a refined dessert brand for the young, and what we built is a pink dream worth photographing. The low-saturation pink is never cloying — it carries a creamy, elevated quality.

Rounded arches and globe lights make the space feel like softly piped cream, plump and gentle.

低饱和的粉不甜腻，反而带着一丝奶油般的高级感；圆润的拱形与球形灯具，让空间像一块被轻轻挤出的奶油，饱满而柔和。
“金属与亚克力的点缀，为甜美注入一点利落的时髦。”

Since 2024

Client · Dessert

“这里还藏着设计师的手绘草图，我们把构思的过程小心地留在空间里，成为一处不经意的巧思。…”

“我们顺着这个「相邻共生」的命题来做——甜品这一侧，用昭和年代温暖的木质、暖黄的灯光与怀旧的陈设，铺出老式的甜；小炒那一侧，则用更明快的色彩与热闹的氛围，接住烟火的香。…”

“材质以木、竹与棉麻为主，素净而不寡淡；一盏茶、一缕香，都在弧形与暖光里找到安放的角落。…”

Our space follows the same logic: restrained, precise, nothing more or less.

JMC Garden

— Foshan Nanhai

Area
75 m²

JMC Garden aspires to be a restaurant that grows out of a garden. We led greenery from the entrance deep into the room, with dark, steady timber anchoring the whole space while plants climb freely above and around every corner, as if the outdoor garden had been brought inside.
Latest delivery 2026

Chushishan 初拾山

Chushishan is a refined Guizhou restaurant, and we wanted to invite the imagery of “mountains” together with Guizhou's earthy warmth into the room. Rough stone and wood echo the plainness of Guizhou's land; rows of lantern pendants, like lights in a mountain village, glow one by one in the evening, warm and steady.

初拾山是一间贵州本味精致餐厅，我们想把「山」的意象与贵州的烟火气，一起请进这间店。


粗犷的石与木，呼应贵州大地的质朴；成排的灯笼吊灯，则像山间村落里的灯火，在夜色里一盏盏亮起，温暖而笃定。

The Shunde store follows a Japanese family woodwork style.

我们想让这间甜品店，像一处被木作温柔包裹的「家」。

Ruchujian 如初见

Ruchujian is a space centered on nourishing sweet soups and buns, and what we want to keep is the gentle warmth of a first meeting. Sweet soup is sweetness simmered slowly; buns are warmth in the palm — so the space leans toward warmth too: the mellow feel of wood, the soft light, layered with the fragrance of simmering soups into a homely sense of rightness.

糖水是慢火熬出的甜，包点是手心里的暖，空间也便往「暖」里走——木的温厚、光的柔暖，与一盅盅糖水的香气叠在一起，构成一种家常的妥帖。

长日的工作， 一间间小店，把作品送往一座座城市， 为更多空间点亮灯光，开一间自己的工作室，收藏每一个亮灯的夜晚 moments of joy...
