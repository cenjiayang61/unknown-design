Guyongxing

Qingtuan · Shanghai Panlong

上海蟠龙天地
35 m²
2026.1

• Photos from the Guyongxing series

A century-old craft, seen again.

顾涌兴，是一家百年手作青团店的品牌升级门店。我们要做的，不是把老店翻成新店，而是让一段延续了百年的手艺，在蟠龙天地这处新场域里，重新被看见。我们把「青团」的青，化作空间里一抹含蓄的点缀；把「手作」的温，落进木的肌理与暖的光里。

Guyongxing is the brand-upgrade store of a century-old handmade qingtuan shop. What we wanted was not to turn an old shop into a new one, but to let a craft that has lasted a hundred years be seen again in the new ground of Panlong Tiandi. We turned the “green” of the qingtuan into a subtle accent in the space, and the warmth of “handmade” into the grain of timber and the glow of light.

老店的气韵被保留下来，化作熟悉的底色；现代的通透与留白，则为它腾出呼吸。灯光温润，像老铺里那盏常亮的灯，照着蒸笼腾起的热气。

The old shop's spirit is kept as a familiar base, while modern transparency and blank space give it room to breathe. The light is warm, like the lamp that always burns in an old shop, shining on the steam rising from the steamer.

这里卖的是一口青团，更是一份被时间善待、又被我们妥善接住的手作温度。

What is sold here is a mouthful of qingtuan, and more than that, a handmade warmth well treated by time and carefully taken up by us.
