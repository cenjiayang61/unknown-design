olayks

Exhibition Booth · Shanghai

上海
120 m²
2024.10

• Photos from the olayks series

A temporary home for a small appliance brand.

olayks 的展台，本质上是在几天之内替一个小家电品牌「搭一个临时的家」。我们把重点放在产品如何被看见：不同功能的产品被安放进各自的「生活场景」里，让参观者一眼就懂「它用在哪里」。

An olayks booth is, in essence, building a small home appliance brand a temporary home in a matter of days. We focused on how the products get seen: each item is placed into its own “life scene,” so a visitor understands at a glance where it belongs.

简洁的体块与留白，让产品成为绝对的主角，色彩克制，只以品牌色做点缀。灯光精准地打在每一件产品上，勾出材质的质感与轮廓。

Clean blocks and generous whitespace make the products the absolute protagonists, with restrained color and only the brand hue as accent. Lighting lands precisely on each product, tracing its material and contour.

展台虽小，动线却流畅——浏览、体验、洽谈，一气呵成。我们要的不是一个喧闹的展位，而是一个能让产品自己说话、让人愿意停下来的安静舞台。

Small as the booth is, the flow is smooth — browse, experience, talk, all in one breath. What we want is not a loud stand, but a quiet stage where products speak for themselves and people are willing to linger.
