Erlun Sweetroom

Dessert · Foshan Chancheng

佛山禅城
150 m²
2026.4

• Photos from the Erlun Sweetroom series

Two storefronts, one twin space.

禅城店最特别的地方，在于它并非一间铺子，而是我们同时租下两个相邻的铺面、一起改造而成的「双生」空间：一边是昭和风情的贰轮甜室，一边是东南亚风味的浪漫小炒，两道门面并肩而立，却各有各的温度。我们顺着这个「相邻共生」的命题来做——甜品这一侧，用昭和年代温暖的木质、暖黄的灯光与怀旧的陈设，铺出老式的甜；小炒那一侧，则用更明快的色彩与热闹的氛围，接住烟火的香。

What makes the Chancheng store special is that it is not a single shop — we rented two adjacent storefronts at once and renovated them together into a “twin” space: on one side the Showa-style Erlun dessert shop, on the other the Southeast-Asian-style Langman stir-fry, two facades standing side by side, each with its own temperature. We took this “neighboring coexistence” idea as the brief — on the dessert side, Showa-era warmth of timber, amber light and nostalgic furnishings lay out an old-fashioned sweetness; on the stir-fry side, brighter colors and a livelier mood catch the fragrance of the wok.

两个铺面之间不做生硬的隔断，而是以材质与光的过渡自然衔接，让客人几步之间，便从一杯甜品，走到一桌小炒。相邻两间，两种温度，正是禅城店最耐人寻味的地方。

There is no abrupt partition between the two; material and light carry the transition naturally, so in a few steps a guest moves from a dessert to a table of stir-fry. Two adjacent shops, two temperatures — that is the most intriguing thing about this store.
