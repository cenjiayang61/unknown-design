Mini Bar

Lounge Bar · Qingyuan

清远
400 m²
2025.12

• Photos from the Mini Bar series

A coffee and music lounge hidden in a country courtyard.

Mini bar 是一间藏在法式复古乡村庭院里的咖啡音乐清吧。它的妙处，正在于「藏」。

Mini bar is a coffee and music lounge hidden in a French-rustic country courtyard. Its charm lies precisely in being hidden.

穿过绿植与斑驳的墙面，才在庭院深处寻到这方小小的天地。我们保留庭院的自然肌理，用复古的砖、木与旧物，堆叠出时间的痕迹；灯光昏黄而温暖，配合舒缓的音乐，把这里调成一处可以发呆整晚的角落。

You pass greenery and mottled walls before finding this small world deep in the yard. We kept the courtyard's natural texture, layering time's traces through reclaimed brick, wood and old objects; the light is dim and warm, and with slow music it tunes the place into a corner where one can daze the whole night.

空间不大，却处处是细节——一扇旧窗、一把藤椅、一盏暖灯，都在低声诉说着慵懒。我们要的不是打卡的热闹，而是一种「寻到」的惊喜：像偶遇一位久违的老友，坐下来，就再不想走。

Small as it is, detail is everywhere — an old window, a rattan chair, a warm lamp, each whispering of idleness. What we want is not the bustle of check-ins, but the delight of discovery: like running into an old friend, and once you sit down, you no longer wish to leave.
