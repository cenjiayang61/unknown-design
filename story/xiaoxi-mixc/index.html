Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Mixc

Dessert · Guangzhou Mixc

广州万象城
65 m²
2026.10

• Photos from the Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Mixc series

One continuous arc, from facade to ceiling.

万象城店的关键词，是一条贯穿始终的弧。弧形木质柜台如一道柔和的波浪，从门头延伸进室内，引导视线与动线自然流淌；吊顶以同源的曲线呼应，消解了商场铺位的方正与冷感。

The keyword of the Mixc store is a single continuous arc. A curved timber counter rolls like a soft wave from the facade inward, guiding the eye and the flow; the ceiling echoes with the same curve, dissolving the boxy coldness of the mall unit.

我们让弧线成为空间的母题——它在转角处圆润收边，在餐位间柔化距离，在灯光里连缀成一条温柔的光带。材质以木与米白为主，暖光打在曲面上，光影随之起伏，空间仿佛有了呼吸。

We made the arc the space's motif — it rounds every corner, softens the distance between seats, and links into a gentle band of light. Timber and warm white dominate, and light falling on curved surfaces makes shadows rise and fall, as if the room breathes.

这里要的是一种被包裹的安全感：不尖锐、不冰冷，像一条缓缓流动的弧线，把每一位客人从商场的喧嚣里温柔地接进来。

What we seek here is a sense of being wrapped: nothing sharp, nothing cold, only a slowly flowing line that gently draws each guest out of the mall's noise.
