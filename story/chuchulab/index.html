chuchulab

Patisserie · Guangzhou Polytrade

广州领展
120 m²
2024.1

• Photos from the chuchulab series

A space as restrained as a Japanese shortcake.

chuchulab 以日式短蛋糕为核心概念。短蛋糕，是一种小而精、恰到好处的甜，我们的空间也依循同样的逻辑——克制、精准、不多不少。

chuchulab is built around the concept of Japanese shortcake — small, refined, and just enough. Our space follows the same logic: restrained, precise, nothing more or less.

干净的白与浅木色打底，像一张等待涂抹的蛋糕胚；几何的线条与灯带，如裱花般利落地勾勒轮廓。吧台作为视觉中心，让「制作」这件事本身被看见，把蛋糕师的手艺变成一场安静的演出。

Clean white and pale wood form the base, like a sponge waiting to be frosted; geometric lines and light strips outline the form as neatly as piping. The counter becomes the visual center, making the act of making itself visible and turning the patissier's craft into a quiet performance.

灯光均匀而明亮，突出每一块短蛋糕的精致。我们不追求繁复，只在乎每一处细节是否「刚刚好」——像短蛋糕一样，体积不大，却足以让人记住它的好。

Light is even and bright, drawing out the delicacy of each slice. We chase no complexity, only whether every detail is exactly right — small in volume, like shortcake, yet enough to be remembered.
