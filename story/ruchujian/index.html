Ruchujian

Sweet Soup & Buns · Guangzhou

广州
75 m²
2025.1

• Photos from the Ruchujian series

The warmth of a first meeting, kept in a shop.

如初见是一间以滋补糖水与包点为核心的空间，我们想留住的，正是「初见」那一眼的温润。糖水是慢火熬出的甜，包点是手心里的暖，空间也便往「暖」里走——木的温厚、光的柔暖，与一盅盅糖水的香气叠在一起，构成一种家常的妥帖。

Ruchujian is a space centered on nourishing sweet soups and buns, and what we want to keep is the gentle warmth of a first meeting. Sweet soup is sweetness simmered slowly; buns are warmth in the palm — so the space leans toward warmth too: the mellow feel of wood, the soft light, layered with the fragrance of simmering soups into a homely sense of rightness.

我们没有刻意做旧，只用素净的材质与得当的留白，让热气与甜香自己去说话。坐在窗边，看一笼包子揭开时腾起的白气，那一刻的踏实，就是这间店最想给的。

We did no deliberate aging, only plain materials and fitting blank space, letting steam and sweetness speak for themselves. Seated by the window, watching the white steam rise as a bamboo steamer opens — that solid comfort is what this shop most wants to give.

初见不惊艳，却足以让人惦记着，再来一次。一盅糖水，一笼包点，皆是暖的。

A first meeting may not startle, but it is enough to be remembered and returned to.
