Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Pavilion

Dessert · Guangzhou Taikoo Li

广州太古里
80 m²
2026.1

• Photos from the Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Pavilion series

A wooden pavilion for tea, folded inside a glass box.

「亭台」，取自东方园林里那一座可停、可望、可休憩的构筑。我们把这份意境落进一间甜品店，用编藤、编绳与木构的经纬，织出一片轻盈的骨架。

“Pavilion” borrows the spirit of an Eastern garden structure — a place to pause, to gaze, to rest. We wove that mood into a dessert shop, using rattan, cord and timber to build a light skeleton.

木作以手工的温度收束空间，藤绳以疏密的变化过滤光线，让空间在通透与围合之间呼吸。软装延续编织的语汇，座椅、灯具皆似被一缕缕编起，既有秩序又不失灵动。

Handcrafted wood brings warmth, while rattan's varying density filters light, letting the space breathe between openness and enclosure. Furnishings continue the woven language, as if chairs and lamps were knitted strand by strand, orderly yet alive.

灯光暖而含蓄，落在藤木的肌理上，投射出斑驳的光影。我们想让人走进来时，像步入一座被藤木温柔包裹的小亭，坐得下来，也望得出去，甜品的甜与空间的静，在这一方亭台里安然相融。

Warm, restrained lighting falls on the grain of rattan and timber, casting dappled shadows. We want people to enter as if stepping into a small pavilion softly wrapped in rattan and wood — able to sit, able to look out, with sweetness and stillness settling together.
