Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Cloud

Dessert · Guangzhou Ali Center

广州阿里中心
75 m²
2025.4

• Photos from the Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Cloud series

A soft cloud for a hard-edged office district.

写字楼片区，工作氛围浓重、冷硬而疏离。我们把「云朵」带进来，让一处法式复古的甜品空间，成为写字楼人群可以短暂抽离的呼吸口。

In an office district defined by heavy, impersonal energy, we introduced a “cloud” — a French-retro dessert space where people can step away and breathe. Light, fluid and curved, the cloud softens the site's rigid lines, so a slice of cake and a good cup of tea can ease the tension of the day.

云朵轻盈、可变、曲态，我们用它去化解场地不可变的硬朗线条，让一件蛋糕的甜与一杯好茶的香，卸下些许紧绷。曲线贯穿家具、灯具与织物，奶油白、原木与藤编为基调，点缀低饱和的粉与灰；餐位错落而非密集，隔断与间距在必要时可并桌，也可各自成席。

Curves run through furniture, lighting and fabric, grounded in cream, raw wood and rattan with low-saturation pink and grey. Seating is staggered rather than dense, with partitions that can merge into shared tables or hold their own.

留出窗边的灵活组合，用更舒适的躺椅延长停留。这里不追求客流的拥挤，只在乎每一个落座的人，能否在柔软的曲线里，找回片刻松弛与治愈。

We kept the window side flexible and chose lounge chairs that invite a longer stay. The goal is never crowd, but whether each guest can find a moment of calm and comfort within the soft curve.
