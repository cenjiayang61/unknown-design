Rice Husk & Malt

Bakery · Foshan Shunde

佛山顺德
200 m²
2024.10

• Photos from the Rice Husk & Malt series

Rice husk and malt, hidden in the name.

「稻壳」与「麦芽」，是这间店名字里就藏着的密码。我们让谷物与丰收的意象，落进空间：麦穗般的暖黄色调，稻壳般质朴的肌理，从材质到灯光，都在低声讲述着田野与谷仓的故事。

“Rice husk” and “malt” are the code hidden in this shop's very name. We let the imagery of grain and harvest settle into the space: a warm yellow tone like ears of wheat, textures as plain as rice husk — from material to light, all quietly telling the story of field and barn.

木的纹理、编织的纹理、略带颗粒感的墙面，像把一缕缕麦香揉进了空间里。灯光如暮色里的麦田，温暖而饱满。

The grain of wood, the weave, the faintly grained walls, seem to knead a thread of malt fragrance into the room. The light is like a wheat field at dusk, warm and full.

我们想营造的，是一种从土地里长出来的踏实与丰足——让人坐在这里，就仿佛闻到麦子成熟的味道，心里也跟着踏实下来。这是名字的由来，也是空间的灵魂。

What we want is a solid, abundant feeling grown out of the earth — so that sitting here, one almost smells ripening wheat and settles with it. This is the origin of the name, and the soul of the space.
