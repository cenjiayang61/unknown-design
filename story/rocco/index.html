Rocco

Patisserie & Café · Zhenjiang

江苏镇江
800 m²
2023.2

• Photos from the Rocco series

Warm Japanese timber, holding a feast of cakes.

露可日式洋果子，是一间八百平米的烘焙与咖啡复合空间，我们想用日式的暖木，托起一场关于「洋果子」的丰盛叙事。大量的木饰面与暖色灯带，把空间铺成一块温润的底；面包展示柜、长吧台与岛台依次展开，让「制作」与「陈列」都成为可被欣赏的风景。

Rocco is an 800-square-meter bakery and coffee space, and we used Japanese warm wood to carry a rich narrative about “Western-style Japanese confectionery.” Layers of timber and warm light strips lay down a mellow base; bread display cases, a long counter and islands unfold in turn, so both making and display become scenery worth watching.

绿植在转角与货架间见缝插针，给这份精致添上呼吸感。白昼里通透明亮，入夜后门头的暖光，又为它披上一层安定的温柔。

Greenery tucks into corners and between shelves, giving the refinement a sense of breath. It is bright and clear by day, and after dark the warm glow of the facade drapes it in a steady tenderness.

我们不在大空间里堆砌，而是让每一处都服务于同一件事——让人停下来，认真吃一块好蛋糕。

We pile nothing up in this large space, but let every part serve one thing — to make people stop and truly enjoy a good cake.
