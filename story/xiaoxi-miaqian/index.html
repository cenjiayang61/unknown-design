Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Miaqian

Dessert · Guangzhou Miaqian Xijie

广州庙前西街
75 m²
2026.5

• Photos from the Xiaoxi Jiazuo · Miaqian series

Hexagonal tiles and brass — memory set into the floor.

庙前西街店，我们把重点落在脚下的六角砖与指尖的黄铜上。六角砖以复古的花色铺开，如同一张延展在地面的几何地毯，界定着落座与行走的节奏；黄铜则出现在把手、灯座与边角，以温润的金属光泽，为空间镀上一层旧时光的质感。

For the Miaqian Xijie shop, we focused on the hexagonal tiles underfoot and the brass at your fingertips. The vintage-patterned tiles spread across the floor like a geometric carpet, setting the rhythm of seating and movement; brass appears on handles, lamp bases and edges, gilding the space with a warm metallic sheen of an older time.

二者一冷一暖、一硬一柔，在地面与细节之间彼此呼应。我们没有用繁复的装饰去铺陈，而是让材质本身成为主角——砖的秩序、铜的光泽、木的温润，共同勾勒出一间克制而有记忆点的甜品店。

One cool and one warm, one hard and one soft, they echo each other between floor and detail. Instead of piling on ornament, we let the materials speak — the order of tile, the luster of brass, the warmth of wood — shaping a restrained yet memorable dessert shop.

走进去，脚下方寸之间，已是一段关于复古与手作的小小叙事。

Step inside, and beneath your feet is already a small narrative of vintage and craft.
