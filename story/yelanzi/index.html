Yelanzi Bakery

Kiln Bakery · Foshan Chancheng

佛山禅城
150 m²
2022.6

• Photos from the Yelanzi Bakery series

An old Lingnan house, with a kiln for a heart.

野篮子窑烤面包屋，藏在一座岭南老建筑里，我们把「窑烤」这件事，做成了整间店的主角。青砖灰瓦的老墙被小心保留下来，成为空间的底色；一炉窑火，则成了这间店的心跳。

Yelanzi is a kiln-baked bakery tucked inside an old Lingnan building, and we made the kiln itself the shop's protagonist. The old brick and grey-tile walls are carefully kept as the space's base, while a kiln fire becomes its heartbeat.

木色与竹编的原色，从展示柜一路铺到座位，暖调的灯光落在砖墙上，烘出一层麦香般的温度。我们不把老房子翻新成另一副模样，而是让它继续「老」下去，再用窑烤的烟火气，为它续上新的生气。

The natural tones of wood and bamboo run from the display counters to the seating, and warm light falling on the brick wall bakes in a temperature like that of wheat. We did not refit the old house into another look, but let it keep aging, and renewed it with the bustle of the oven.

面包出炉时的焦香，混着老墙的土气，正是这间店最动人的地方——手艺、老屋与麦香，在这里彼此成全。

The charred fragrance of bread rising from the oven, mingled with the earthiness of the old walls, is the most moving thing here — craft, old house and wheat, each completing the other.
