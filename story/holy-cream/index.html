HOLY CREAM

Dessert · Foshan Chancheng

佛山禅城
75 m²
2024.12

• Photos from the HOLY CREAM series

A pink dream worth photographing.

HOLY CREAM 是面向年轻人的精致甜品品牌，我们想做的是一个「会让人想拍照发圈」的粉色梦境。低饱和的粉不甜腻，反而带着一丝奶油般的高级感；圆润的拱形与球形灯具，让空间像一块被轻轻挤出的奶油，饱满而柔和。

HOLY CREAM is a refined dessert brand for the young, and what we built is a pink dream worth photographing. The low-saturation pink is never cloying — it carries a creamy, elevated quality.

金属与亚克力的点缀，为甜美注入一点利落的时髦。灯光刻意调成柔焦般的暖粉，映在人脸与甜品上都格外柔和。

Rounded arches and globe lights make the space feel like softly piped cream, plump and gentle. Touches of metal and acrylic sharpen the sweetness with a modern edge.

这里没有沉重的装饰，只有恰到好处的甜——我们相信，年轻人在乎的不是堆砌，而是一眼就能记住、愿意为它停留的那份轻盈与好看。

Lighting is deliberately soft-focused into a warm pink, flattering both faces and desserts. There is no heavy ornament, only the right amount of sweet — because we believe what young people want is not accumulation, but a lightness and beauty they remember at first sight and are willing to stay for.
