soho !

Café · Foshan Nanhai

佛山南海
75 m²
2023.5

• Photos from the soho ! series

One color sets the tone: mint green.

soho ! 是一间咖啡轻食简餐空间，我们用一个颜色，替它定了调——薄荷绿。这抹绿清新、年轻、带一点俏皮，从门头一路漫进室内，成了整间店最鲜明的记忆点。

soho ! is a coffee and light-meal space, and we set its tone with a single color — mint green. Fresh, youthful and a little playful, this green flows from the facade deep into the interior, becoming the shop's clearest memory point.

我们用白与浅木去衬托它，让薄荷绿跳出来却不刺眼；再以圆润的家具、柔和的弧线中和它的清冷，让空间显得轻快又亲切。灯光通透，适合白天的一杯咖啡、一份简餐。

We use white and pale wood to set it off, so the mint stands out without glaring; rounded furniture and soft curves temper its coolness, keeping the space light and welcoming. The light is clear, suited to a daytime coffee or a simple meal. Nothing heavy here — only comfort, like a refreshing sip of mint that relaxes you the moment you walk in.

这里不讲究厚重，只讲究「舒服」——像一口清爽的薄荷，让人一走进来就放松下来。一个颜色就能被记住，这正是 soho ! 想要的那种轻快。

One color, remembered at once: that is the lightness soho ! is after.
